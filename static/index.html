Failure Analysis & Caveats

Honest scientific evaluation: where the system stumbles, why, and how to fix it.

Mandatory Audit: "What is Misleading About My Headline Number?"

Our headline 99.7% intent accuracy looks impressive, but is inherently deceptive if viewed in isolation:

  • Class Imbalance Masking: 78% of Twitter volume is concentrated in three categories (hardware, app, OS). A model can completely fail on rare classes (like billing fraud or angry complaints) while still boasting 90%+ accuracy.
  • Coverage vs. Safety Trade-off: A False Auto-Handle Rate of 13.0% was achieved at 23.0% coverage. If we forced 100% automation (like Baseline 1), FAHR explodes to 41.0%!
  • Retrieval Similarity Drift: Without cross-encoder reranking, abstract financial terms can score high cosine similarities while describing completely different operational issues (e.g. gift card errors matching bank authorization holds).

The Top 5 Empirical Failure Modes (From Golden Set Runs)

Customer Input: "Battery drain"

Actual Output: Auto-handled with standard Battery Health guide (Confidence: 89.7%).

Expected: Escalate or ask for device model and iOS version.

Root Cause: High classifier posterior on terse queries due to closed-set softmax without length regularization.

Fix: Add input word-count rule ($< 4$ words triggers clarification/escalation).

Customer Input: "Notes app wiped all my folders after the last update!! 5 years of work gone HELP ME PLEASE"

Actual Output: Auto-handled with camera force-restart troubleshooting.

Expected: Mandatory escalation to senior support for iCloud backup recovery.

Root Cause: Semantic embeddings capture the app name but miss the emotional distress and severity of data loss.

Fix: Distress keyword pre-filter ("wiped", "5 years of work", "help me please") forcing immediate escalation.

Customer Input: "Phone is completely frozen on black screen with spinning gear wheel for 6 hours"

Actual Output: Auto-handled with Netflix app update suggestion.

Expected: Recovery Mode guide or hardware repair routing.

Root Cause: Word "frozen" aligned with app freezing rather than OS SpringBoard boot-loop failure.

Fix: Hardware state classifier detecting "black screen" + "spinning wheel" patterns.

Customer Input: "I was charged $89.99 for an annual app I never purchased! Refund my money immediately!"

Actual Output: Auto-handled with general self-service refund portal link.

Expected: Escalate to billing fraud investigation team.

Root Cause: Treating all billing inquiries under one broad bucket without distinguishing active disputes from FAQ queries.

Fix: Dollar amount threshold ($> $20) and dispute intent bifurcation.

Customer Input: "Gift card says already redeemed but balance is zero!"

Actual Output: Provided explanation of debit card temporary authorization holds.

Expected: Gift card receipt upload guidance and fraud escalation.

Root Cause: Vector search high similarity (0.901) based on shared vocabulary ("balance", "zero") despite completely different subject matter.

Fix: Cross-encoder reranker with lexical entity verification.
